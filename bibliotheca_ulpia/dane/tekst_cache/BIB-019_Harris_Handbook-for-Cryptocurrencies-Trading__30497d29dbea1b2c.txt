      Chapter 1  Altcoin Fundamental Analysis and the Importance of On-Chain Metrics - Will Your Investment Pay Off?      
            
      “Bitcoin will do to banks  what email did to the postal industry”    
      Rick Falkvinge     
            
      Analysis is the trump card traders use to determine the validity of an investment. Under the term validity, we distinguish two main speculative macro areas: the first focuses on the long-term buying and selling of cryptocurrencies, the second on short-term price fluctuations (often reduced to a time window of just 24 hours) to predict possible price changes. In case number 1, those who bet on cryptocurrencies in order not to sell in the short to medium term (long position) will evaluate the potential of a coin or project as a whole (white paper, native or non-native blockchain, reputation, management and governance , marketing and token sponsorship, benefits, long-term goals, etc.). This set of metrics is called fundamental analysis and is the topic of this chapter. On the other hand, traders interested in predicting short-term fluctuations in value (short position) will resort to various indicators: moving averages, TA data, charts and candlestick diagrams, but also trend lines. This study is called technical analysis and requires an approach diametrically opposed to the above.     
      Leaving aside the basics of Technical Analysis (TA) let's take a closer look at the components of Fundamental Analysis (FA). As mentioned earlier, altcoins vary enormously in terms of blockchain size and security, market cap, token type, and long-term projects. In addition, each cryptocurrency is based on a different reward and verification system (PoW or PoS), which further complicates the analysis phase for a novice trader. In any case, we recommend that you don't get discouraged and throw in the towel at the slightest difficulty:                     an altcoin's many, many variables are an unbeatable asset. In the vast majority of cases, many professional traders have long underestimated the usefulness of properly conducted fundamental analysis. However, in our opinion it is not possible to take your investments to the next level without having an understanding of crypto projects as a whole.     
      Whenever you decide to take your first steps into uncharted territory, fundamental analysis will help you find a way out.    
      The factors we want to draw your attention to are the intrinsic properties of the currency, the  exchange  platforms that allow tracking price fluctuations, sentiment marketing dynamics and the development of native tokens (white papers, websites, wallets, validation protocols etc.).     
      Let's proceed in order.     
            
      The characteristics of a cryptocurrency      
      If bitcoin is trending, some altcoins are no different. A conscious diversification of your wallet within the crypto universe is mandatory by buying versatile and heterogeneous altcoins - like GameFI or the decentralized finance system DeFi, which we will discuss in the next sections. The advantage is twofold: on the one hand, you can get acquainted with the most promising current blockchain applications and, on the other hand, you have the opportunity to compensate for possible financial losses if the value of your Bitcoin/Satoshi should fall drastically. Be warned, however: among altcoins that rise, peak, and fall daily, the vast majority of suggestions are sure to leave you out in the cold. Consciously investing in a cryptocurrency that piques your curiosity means putting the currency through a long, long fundamental analysis. Without a thorough preliminary study, you risk losing your fiat money [1]  Convert to cryptocurrencies with very little growth potential. On the other hand, a thorough understanding of the current market will help you to significantly reduce the risk of betting on a crypto project that fizzles out. Regardless of the qualitative and quantitative factors discussed in this chapter, we would like to draw your attention to two elements that are of paramount importance: a currency 's  coin supply and the nature of the blockchain network  .     
      The term supply refers to the supply of the cryptocurrency we are interested in.     
      There are three types of supply that we can base our analysis on:     
     
       Total Supply  – The total supply of a cryptocurrency corresponds to the number of coins (tokens) in circulation at the time of investment. Assets reserved or allocated according to the rules set out in the project's white paper are also taken into account. In practice, the total supply is the sum of coins issued minus coins destroyed and/or unredeemable by users of the blockchain network.     
       Circulating Supply  – The circulating supply is - as the name suggests - the amount of tokens that are publicly available at the time of investment.     
       Maximum Supply  – This ultimately corresponds to the maximum supply that is generated and made available on the market. In other words, reaching the Maximum Supply will interrupt the mining process. This determines the extraction and production of new tokens according to the rules of the blockchain and its validation system.     
     
      In general, the smaller  the supply of cryptocurrencies, the higher their value  . And indeed, the constant market fluctuations faced by  crypto traders  are due to the increasing number of tokens being mined, circulated and offered for sale. Of the forms of supply mentioned above, the one we recommend you look at financially is circulating supply. The reason for this is simple: in contrast to the semi-static values for the total and maximum supply, the circulating supply has a large impact on the capital you want to move. It is recommended to stay away from all altcoins that are  under 6 digits or over 10-11 digits  . In the first case, you risk investing in an unstable project, which is often launched on the  crypto market for the developer's pleasure . In the second case, you enter a crowded market whose liquidity drops drastically at the slightest fluctuation.     
      Also, don't underestimate the importance of Maximum Supply. This allows you to predict if and when your cryptocurrency will go through a major inflationary crisis. In other words, this value sheds light on the growth potential of a cryptocurrency in a transparent and very accurate way. Indeed, the average cost of an asset is closely related to its  desirability  and  scarcity  . In general, at Mindful Finance, we assume the following relationship: if the circulating supply of a currency is less than 25% of the maximum supply of the first block generation, it is very likely to invest in an embryonic-stage cryptocurrency (whose value increases dramatically as the circulating supply increases could increase, especially considering the small initial investment). If you are interested in a currency that has been issued for a longer period of time, you should focus on cryptocurrencies that have a high Circulating Supply to Maximum Supply ratio. The reason? Inflation will now be negligible, especially for traders looking to make long-term profits (  long position  ).     
      Ideally, the best solution would be to invest in a newly launched currency with a high maximum supply rate (more than 2/3 of total supply). However, the tokens in question are rough diamonds, which are very difficult to find in the sea of altcoins that hit the market every day.     
      It's worth a try!    
      The second golden feature of a successful cryptocurrency relates to the block  structure  . This term refers to the reward systems for mining new tokens and the average time it takes to create a new block. We assume you know something about the dynamics that keep a blockchain network alive: every crypto transaction is validated by active users of the network in exchange for rewards. With the help of some basic mathematical operations, we can roughly predict the value of the circulating supply over a given period of time, thus understanding the inflation rate of the cryptocurrency.     
      To better explain the concept, let's start from the end and analyze the block structure of the famous DOGE coin. The reward systems are as follows  [2]  :     
      Block 1-99,000: 0-1,000,000 Dogecoin     
      Block 100,000 — 144,999: 0-500,000 Dogecoin     
      Block 145,000 — 199,999: 250,000 Dogecoins     
      Block 200,000 — 299,999: 125,000 Dogecoins     
      Block 300,000 — 399,999: 62,500 Dogecoin     
      Block 400,000 — 499,999: 31,250 Dogecoin     
      Block 500,000 — 599,999: 15,625 Dogecoins     
      Block 600,000+: 10,000 Dogecoins     
            
      Considering that the creation of a new block - in  Time Block jargon  - has an approximate value of 60 seconds, the average number of blocks per hour is about 60 (1440 in 24 hours). The reward system therefore breaks down significantly over time. From block 600,000, the rewards decrease dramatically, indicating an increase in the average value of 1 DOGE. As an investor, it would not have made sense to convert their crypto capital into DOGE Coin in the initial phase of mining.     
      The reason for this?     
      High rewards encourage miners to mine more and more tokens. As the Circulating Supply increases in record time, it is inevitable that the price of the currency will become deflationary relative to its potential value.     
      Using this example, we can shed light on the potential price fluctuations of a cryptocurrency. Analyzing the structure of the blockchain - based on the  white paper  and the relationship between  mining  and  reward  - allows you to distinguish between potentially profitable speculative investments and cryptocurrencies from which you cannot get much financial benefit. If the mining reward decreases over time, the cryptocurrency is likely to increase in value and desirability. With fixed rewards, on the other hand, one can expect the price of the currency to remain almost unchanged over time (or even drop drastically if the community isn't seriously interested in the tokens and the  philosophy  of the project).     
      As a speculator, you should always keep one very important rule in mind:  rarity is gold in your hands and in your crypto wallet when demand is high.  Investing time and money in static projects will make you lose confidence in your trading abilities, especially if you are trying to make money from a  long position .    
            
      Market capitalization        
      Without a doubt, one of our favorite metrics is the market cap of the cryptocurrency we want to invest in. After dealing with the value of Supply and the characteristics of the blockchain network, you should also deal with the  market capitalization . According to the official Coinbase definition , the latter is “the total value of all coins that have been mined. It is calculated by multiplying the number of currencies in circulation by the current market price of a single currency  ”. In other words, market capitalization is the total value (in USD) of all shares of a company or, in the case of cryptocurrencies, all tokens that have already been mined on a decentralized blockchain network.     
      And if you're wondering how market cap affects fundamental analysis (FA) of a cryptocurrency, remember that its numerical value reflects the likelihood that that currency will withstand market fluctuations over time.Well, we suggest that you don't take these statements literally; remember that it is the job of the professional trader to approach fundamental analysis with the right level of critical thinking. Even Bitcoin, the cryptocurrency with the highest market cap on record, is not (and has not been) exempt from the roller coaster ride that is keeping investors on their toes. So, market capitalization is just one of many characteristics to examine to familiarize yourself with a complex and unpredictable market. While it's true that small-cap cryptocurrencies are more exposed to the vagaries of the market - meaning they can offer traders exponential gains or significant losses - it's important to remember that even today's big players are not immune to shocks and major setbacks are.     
      Before we proceed, it's important to note that market cap is often referred to as 'supply outstanding' and 'fully diluted supply'. This information will help you read charts and tables more carefully.     
      Why does market cap play such an important role in altcoin investing?     
      The reason is quickly explained.     
      Let's imagine we want to invest our capital in cryptocurrency A or cryptocurrency B. After analyzing the coin supply and the nature of the blockchain network, we are still very undecided on what to do. On the one hand, Crypto A has a circulating supply of 500,000 tokens that are sold at an average price of $1. The market capitalization is thus 500,000 USD. Crypto B, on the other hand, has a circulating inventory of about 100,000 coins, which are traded by users at an approximate price of $2. The market capitalization of Crypto B is thus 200,000 USD. This example sheds light on a very strange behavior of the crypto market: although crypto A is selling at a lower price than crypto B, its market cap is drastically higher than that of its “rival”. Because of this, the circulating supply plays a key role in detecting winning currencies, which is necessary to differentiate your wallet in a smart and conscious way.     
      But be careful:  market capitalization is a constantly changing number  . The volatility of a cryptocurrency can trigger very strong fluctuations, especially during the Bitcoin season [3]  .     
      In general, we recommend that you “read” market cap data as follows:     
     
       Small capitalization cryptocurrencies  are more exposed to unpredictable and uncontrollable market fluctuations. Capitalization is less than $1 billion.     
       Mid-cap cryptocurrencies  range from $1 billion to $10 billion. Numerous & promising cryptocurrencies are the ones that we at Mindful Finance are used to doing in-depth fundamental analysis on.     
       Large-cap cryptocurrencies  have a capitalization in excess of $10 billion. The already mentioned Bitcoin and Ethereum belong to this category. The advantage? Traders find long periods of stability and market liquidity very promising: the currencies involved are more reliable, more solid and safer.     
     
      We would like to remind you once again that we should not oversimplify the information in question. Market cap is very useful for comparing two altcoins, but it needs to be accompanied by a thorough study of the cryptocurrency's trends, seasonality, and its overall financial health.     
            
      Market Capitalization Vs. Fully Diluted Market Capitalization      
      Dear reader,  among the fundamental analysis tools related to the traditional market capitalization, we would like to draw your attention to the  fully diluted market capitalization  . Its goal is to estimate the approximate future value of a crypto asset (and more).And indeed,  Fully Diluted Market Capitalization  is a concept derived from the traditional stock market, where the value of a company is assessed in a scenario where all of its securities are appropriately converted into shares. Let's apply the same concept to the cryptoeconomic universe and take the Bitcoin mining model as an example; we know  the maximum supply of BTC is 21 million, right?  To calculate the  fully diluted market cap  , we need to multiply the price of 1 BTC by 21 million, bringing us to the extraordinary figure of nearly $400 trillion. Please note that the metric in question is applicable to all altcoins on the market with a fixed number of minings over time. While the high volatility prevents us from providing accurate and stable numbers over time, the  fully diluted market cap offers  an undeniable FA advantage: it allows us to understand if the  alternative coin  we are interested in is trading in a undervalued or overvalued in a given market phase.     
      The reason for this?    
      There are extremely profitable altcoins that have attempted to increase their desirability and/or rarity by destroying a significant percentage of the circulating supply. This strategy aims to fight the inflationary tendency of many  alternative coins  in an “aggressive” way. The process of currency depreciation is known as “  coin burn  ” and has a huge impact on the value of the  fully diluted market cap  , as Maximum Supply affects the cap estimate. This approach is very useful for analyzing the robustness of older cryptocurrencies, i.e. those that have been around for at least 12 months. Just as a company that is listing a lot of stocks leads to more  inflationary pressures  - by lowering the average value of an asset - the  Fully Diluted Market Cap is  useful for examining the potential downside of an altcoin saturating the market ( with the consequent loss of value).     
      In other words, a large gap between market cap (relative to circulating supply) and fully diluted market cap (derived from maximum supply) is a good sign. This means that users have confidence in the future potential of the currency. Conversely, an increase in the number of coins in circulation that only slightly impacts the  fully diluted market cap should  alarm the most discerning investors. It is likely that the altcoin will face a strong inflationary and bearish trend.     
            
      The Exchanges - Where and Why Should You Buy Your Altcoin?      
      We know that: The nature of exchange platforms seems to have nothing to do with the value of a cryptocurrency. However, with a properly conducted fundamental analysis, the tools provided by crypto trading platforms cannot be disregarded. We recommend the following:  Binance, Poloniex, KiCoin, Livecoin, CryptoBridge, CoinExchange, Bittrex  and  Cryptopia  .Each of the eight exchanges mentioned above offers a comprehensive package of services, tools and instruments that can be used to analyze market trends. In particular, Bittrex and Cryptopia present in a logical and rigorous manner all the basic information that needs to be evaluated before opening a new position.     
      Let's go in order.     
      In general, a cryptocurrency A behaves as follows: It is introduced to the crypto market at a low price on smaller exchange platforms (e.g. CryptoBridge and CoinExchange). As the circulating supply and market cap increase, our currency  may  end up on Poloniex and KuCoin before topping the market on Bittrex -  if all goes well  . Suffice it to say that the lowest market cap cryptocurrencies on  Binance  never fall below $30 billion; those on Bittrex never fall below $2 billion. Conversely, some Cryptopia altcoins are capitalized with no more than 300k USD, so accumulating your profitable alternative coins should always start with CryptoBridge, CoinExchange and so on.     
      The reason for this?     
      Low-volume exchanges allow you to diversify your wallet without investing large amounts of capital up front, with the risk that the currency you are interested in will leave you out in the rain. However, diversifying your wallet on a low budget is extremely profitable in the long term: you can be sure that one out of the twenty to thirty altcoins you buy will climb the exchange platforms as its value and market cap will increase over the long term. Just buy small fragments of the currencies you are interested in and follow their development. If you've done your fundamental analysis efficiently and rigorously, you stand a high chance of making significant profits on the  newbie altcoins you bet on.    
      Of course, this strategy works in the  long position and has nothing to do with short trading  supported by technical analysis . Trust us when we say that your patience will pay off financially - as long as you have enough stamina and persistence to follow your altcoin's market swings.     
            
      The role of market utility and diversification      
      Dear reader,  we would like to start this paragraph with a warning: be wary of purported crypto gurus posting «  the 10 altcoins to invest in  » or «  updated list of top cryptocurrencies  » style articles and videos on “ Go click catch. While this material may be useful for uncovering interesting investment opportunities, it trivializes the actual  market utility  of the currency.The term “  market utility  ” refers to the futuristic identity of any initiative based on blockchain technology. Before you point your finger at any random CoinmarketCap altcoin and amass a significant percentage of it on exchange platforms, you should ask yourself: «  What is the novelty that this initiative brings that interests me? What makes the alternative coin in question so special? What does it offer that sets it apart from the hundreds or thousands of competitors on the market?  ».     
      As superfluous as these considerations may seem - after all, you are still all about buying and reselling cryptocurrencies to make a profit - understanding the market utility is a factor in maintaining the integrity and desirability of those stored in your  crypto wallet  get currencies. Also in the field of decentralized finance (De-Fi) there are experiments and initiatives that are 100% decentralized and aim to solve problems common to a significant number of users. For this reason, interesting solutions - such as Enjin (ENJ) - have emerged that address the needs of  non-fungible token enthusiasts  in a new and avant-garde way by offering ready-to-use kits to develop new NFTs.     
      The mechanism is simple: identify a problem and offer a solution.     
      Ditto for the altcoin Solana - Ethereum's direct " competitor  " - which aims to create a revolution in the field of decentralized value exchange, the use of smart contracts and NFT projects (artistic and otherwise).  Why should users choose Solana  ? For its ability to differentiate itself from Vitalik Buterin's platform by achieving unprecedented levels of blockchain network speed and dynamics (processing around 50,000 transactions per second, compared to Ethereum's 15).     
      Not bad, right?    
      In short, anyone deciding to invest in a crypto project in the early days should ask themselves  what makes the offering in question unique and/or desirable  . It's easy to go for " naked  " gains. In the absence of an accumulation plan based on the actual solidity of the crypto initiative, you risk putting your trust in many (too many) negligible tokens. Today's market has taught us that the winners among altcoins were (and still are) those that bring some novelty, relief, or substantial revolution to market dynamics. To find the alternative coins you are interested in, you should focus on the white paper and the goals set by the development team: Are the goals of the project communicated clearly and transparently? Or are they mouthing words that have little to do with the actual meaning of the altcoin? Do they offer guarantees? If so, are they realistic?     
      Remember that anyone -  yes, including you reading this guide  - has the opportunity to create a token and put it on the market, perhaps hoping to fool a beginner. At Mindful Finance, we would like to warn you about altcoin and de-fi projects that are repetitive, empty, unrealistic or lack social and/or online communication. Instead, we suggest you check out some particularly interesting experiments on Coinbase by following this  link [4]  click; a good starting point to familiarize yourself with the special features of more than 110 projects. Over time, you will be able to spot a potentially profitable offer and avoid perceived  coin scams  .    
      So remember, winning currency A) solves a problem in innovative ways and B) appeals to the widest possible audience to create a long-term, loyal community.     
      Have fun researching!    
           
      Advanced fundamental analysis - on-chain parameters save your wallet      
      We assume that you already master basic fundamental analysis (FA) with the precision and accuracy of a professional crypto trader. In this short altcoin post, we would like to go a few steps further and guide you through a "hidden world": a set of excellent metrics and parameters that have revolutionized our approach and investment experience. We are of course referring to on-chain analytics. The latter is an advanced branch of fundamental analysis and aims to meet the needs of speculators with specific factors provided by the blockchain itself. Yes, because blockchain technology is anything but “  privatized  ”. But on the contrary. It allows anyone to dive into the financial dynamics and behavior of other users with a click of the mouse and a (certain) reading strategy. Below, we recommend you to log into the  Binance Reasearch  homepage and first click on the “  Insights & Analysis  ” panel in the top right corner to view the latest general reports (released in the current week/month). These are a good starting point for finding information about trending altcoins. Finally, enter the name of the altcoin that interests you in the search bar (also at the top right) and click on the currency's personal page.

      Valuable Alternative to Altcoin or a Scam?    
            
      ICO – Initial Coin Offering       
      An  Initial Coin Offering  (ICO) is a fundraising strategy used by research teams looking to implement projects based on blockchain and/or cryptocurrencies. The initiators generate a certain number of tokens using blockchain technology and distribute the new coins to users who decide to support them at an early stage. The mechanism in question is not so different from the classic  crowdfunding  that we have long become accustomed to through the Internet: whoever decides to participate in the project receives some “premium” benefits and, in the case of the cryptoeconomic universe, one Number of tokens that can be used/exchanged immediately or in the future.     
      For context, it is worth remembering that the initial coin offering  boom dates back to 2014.  The practice in question has in fact favored the financing of Ethereum: the idea of the very young Russian programmer Vitalik Buterin was subsequently successfully “copied” by thousands of futuristic companies looking for secure, anonymous and meritocratic systems for sourcing funds were. In this regard, the absence of intermediaries who monitor and legitimize the funding of companies plays a key role: no commissions paid to third-party banks, maximum transparency, active participation of  user-supporters  and an excellent long-term strategy for crypto fund accumulation through the community engagement.     
      A word of caution though: don't fall into the trap of thinking that accumulating tokens is equivalent to buying assets/property from the developer (as is the case in the traditional market). In the vast majority of cases, the coins reach your active address in the chain to then be sold and/or traded.     
      If the project is the launch of a native blockchain that has not yet been successfully released, the coins will be issued and distributed via an already active and working network (such as Ethereum, NEO, Stellar, Waves or NEM). Only when the official network is ready and tested will the initial tokens be placed back onto the blockchain of the project you support. Among the most common practices is the use of  smart contracts  , which are specifically designed to exchange assets. The term “  Smart Contract  ” describes a digital instrument that enables classic contractual agreements between two or more users to be made and protected in a secure, decentralized and uncomplicated/understandable way. In today's crypto economy world, smart contracts are programs and/or applications based on the blockchain that work even when there is no trust between the parties involved in the agreement (  trustless method  ). To be successful, the smart contract has a set of  predefined rules  and  validation  conditions that are set at the computer coding stage; the contract is  only  validated in the network if all predefined requirements are successfully met.     
      The advantage is obvious: users can make agreements from one end of the world to the other via the blockchain, without the need for an intermediary and in a climate where there is no mutual trust. A revolution sanctioned by Vitalik Buterin on his Ethereum platform. Smart contracts are processed by the  Ethereum Virtual Machine  (EVM): The central computer ensures the execution of the code strings encapsulated in the smart contract contract. As a result, startups and CEOs looking for fast, secure, and functional ways to distribute ICO tokens have turned to issuing their own currencies using smart contracts. This practice is widespread in the Ethereum environment: there are estimated to be over 200,000 tokens of different types, many using the ERC-20 smart contract system (default type).     
      Another thing to keep in mind is that an ICO is usually announced well in advance, accompanied by a white paper and all the information you need to decide  if and when  to get involved in the initiative. In general, the  Initial Coin Offering  team will determine the time frame that the ICO will remain eligible, the number of tokens that will be available for sale at the time of launch, the popularity of the project, and the technical data to be included in the project document . Users who want to take part in the initiative are free to transfer a certain amount of Bitcoin or Ethereum - other altcoins are rarely accepted - to the  Founders ' address  . In return, participants provide an address where they receive tokens, or in some cases they can redeem their reward directly at the address used for the initial payment.       Wallets      
      Wallets are the undisputed protagonists of the cryptoeconomic financial system. Without secure, anonymous, and decentralized wallets, we would continue to transact with traditional credit cards, exposing our data and money to intermediaries, identity theft, and potential digital threats. This is exactly why wallets are an essential part of fundamental analysis. In our Bitcoin and blockchain guide, we've already covered the many different types of wallets: from  hardware wallets  - tangible purses the size of a thumb drive that you carry with you - to the many, many  software wallets  (  web wallet, desktop wallet and mobile wallet  ) to the simplest and most straightforward of them all: the  paper wallet . The latter is a piece of paper on which your wallet addresses, public key and private key are printed in the form of QR codes. To validate a crypto transaction, all you have to do is scan the codes with any cutting-edge high-tech device and wait for the crypto movement to be transferred to the distributed ledger.  Fast and easy!    
      In general, crypto wallets are often divided into two macro categories: hot wallets and cold wallets.     
     
       A  hot wallet  is a wallet whose operation depends on an internet connection (e.g.  web wallet, desktop wallet and mobile wallet  ). The advantage? You can deposit your funds quickly and efficiently, use the fundamental and technical analysis tools provided by the platform of your choice (e.g. Binance) and keep track of the total balance of all addresses connected to your wallet.     
       The term  cold wallet  refers to a wallet that does not need to be connected to the internet to function. Such is the case with the hardware wallets and paper wallets mentioned above, whose  physical media  are sufficient to deposit and move your cryptocurrencies without any intermediary platforms. your strong point?  security  . Cold wallets are resistant to all common cyber attacks targeting custodial platforms.     
     
      The question arises:  When should you choose a hot wallet and when should you choose a cold wallet?

Among the services that can help you circumvent the hassles of using a hardware wallet, we recommend looking at  Binance DEX  – the original Binance platform that tracks cryptocurrency buying and selling through a distributed ledger that similar to how any cryptocurrency (especially Bitcoin) works without storing users' data, funds and information on the server.       01 - Moving Average (MA)    
      While the altcoin market is intangible and 100% digital, it is also very noisy and chaotic. In this regard, the moving average allows investors to step away from the micro swings of the market to see  the overall trend  of a currency. Currency data is filtered by MA to give a clearer and more reliable picture of the phase the currency is in (e.g. bull or bear).     
      Please note  : Because the moving average analysis is  lagged (ie based on past data), the MA is defined as lagging  . In other words, the indicator in question aims to shed light on the current/existing tendencies, both based on the financial cycles that have just ended and on the basis of information about previous prices.    
      Some of the most popular moving averages for advanced trading include the Simple Moving Average (MA or SMA) and the Exponential Moving Average (EMA).     
     
       The  Simple Moving Average  takes the average value of a crypto asset over a period of time and determines its price by calculating the average. An example? Let's imagine tracking the SMA of a cryptocurrency A over the last 10 days: after adding the dominant costs, we divide the total by the number of days (10). In the case of a 3-day period where the highest prices were $45, $78 and $56, we calculate the SMA as follows: 45 + 78 + 56 / 3 = $69.6. The procedure is extremely intuitive and reaches its highest effectiveness when the time frame of the analysis is longer - ie when trading a  long position  .

       The  Exponential Moving Average  (EMA) uses a different mechanism as it takes more account of  recent  price action in the timeframe set by the trader. The advantage for  short position  investors is considerable: the results are more dynamic and accurate than the current market situation. The mathematical formula required to calculate the EMA is as follows:     
     
            
         
            
      T  is the price of the cryptocurrency at the time the Exponential Moving Average is calculated.    
      Y  is the price of the cryptocurrency on the day before applying the formula.     
      N  is the total number of days considered in the EMA.    
      K  is a fixed value that can be calculated as follows: 2 / (total number of days + 1)     
      In addition, experienced traders use moving averages to determine which cyclical phase of the market they are interested in. So if a 100-day altcoin SMA is  lower  than a 200-day altcoin SMA, then the cryptocurrency is in a bear market and traders are being pushed to sell. The reason? The recent price is lower than the last 200-day price, suggesting that a slow trend reversal is just beginning.       
      02 – Relative Strength Index (RSI)    
      The Relative Strength Index (RSI) measures price swings relative to a cryptocurrency's supply and demand, using a 14-day or 14-hour time frame (depending on whether the trader is  long  or  short  ). The information derived from the RSI is represented numerically by a value between 0 and 100. In general, the analysis standards are as follows:     
     
       When the RSI value crosses 70, the cryptocurrency you are interested in is overbought and in a strong uptrend.     
       When the RSI value is below 30, the asset is oversold and dominated by a downtrend.     
     
      However, one should not think that this data accurately reflects the financial behavior of investors. Like most indicators used for successful technical analysis, the Relative Strength Index can produce misleading and fictitious results. The reason for this is to be found in its nature: a  momentum indicator  . The latter measures the  rate  at which the altcoin's price changes over time. If it rises, it is possible that speculators' interest will increase; when it falls, investors are likely to look for other trading opportunities and the market is gradually reducing its liquidity.     
      Please Note  : Pay attention to the time frame that the Relative Strength Index examines. When set to 7 days, it is undoubtedly more sensitive to micro price changes than an analysis looking at 14 days (or more). For short-term research (5-7 days), we advise you to change the interpretation levels as follows: 80 + (bullish phase) and 20 - (bearish phase).  This allows you to reduce the impact of incomplete and/or erroneous signals that could throw you off course.

      03 – Stochastic RSI (StochRSI)    
      The term Stochastic RSI refers to an indicator derived from the simple Relative Strength Index and as such oscillates below or above a center line. Consequently, its interpretation is among the simplest in technical analysis. It was first theorized in the 1994 book  The New Technical Trader  by Stanley Kroll and Tushar Chande and is used in traditional trading contexts as well as in the cryptocurrency and altcoin world.     
      How does this work? The result of your TA gives you an approximate numerical score - derived from the mathematical formula of the Stochastic Oscillator - which gives a general view of investors' financial behavior. In the vast majority of cases, the StochRSI values are between 0 and 1 (with a center line of 0.5) or alternatively between 0 and 100 (with a center line of 50).     
      At Mindful Finance, we encourage you to increase the reliability of your technical analysis by simultaneously reading the aforementioned Simple Moving Average (SMA) and the StochRSI derived from the Relative Strength Index.     
      For number jugglers, the formula for the Stochastic Oscillator is as follows:     
            
      StochRSI = (Current RSI - Lowest RSI over 14 days) / (Highest RSI - Lowest RSI)    
           
      Please note that time periods can be customized by choosing months, days, hours or even minutes.     
      Now let's focus on the interpretation of the StochRSI: the aim of this indicator is to spot  trend reversals, potential entry points and possible exit points  where you can close your position. A reading of around 0.2 or less suggests that the desired crypto asset is oversold by other investors. Conversely, a StochRSI reading of 0.8 or higher indicates high trader interest. When the StochRSI fluctuates around 0.5 and the median line for several days in a row, it means that the behavior of the speculators is almost consistent/stable as it follows a gradual uptrend gradually converging towards the Bull Market point (from 0 ,8) moved towards. Obviously, a reading of 0.5 slowly moving towards 0.2 indicates the initiation of a downtrend (bear market).     
      Dear Reader,  Since the Stochastic RSI is an indicator secondarily derived from the Relative Strength Index, one needs to trade with due caution. The risk of falling into error is real: false signals can convey a market picture that is inconsistent with your actual behavior. Tip #1: Always apply an SMA to your StochRSI to compare medians. In most cases, most advanced trading sites use a 3-day SMA by default to assist you with your TA.     
      This is the case with  Liquid  . The red box refers to a 14-day period with a 3-day SMA. The value of the Stochastic RSI ranges from 0 to 100.     
            
      04 – Bollinger Bands (BB, Bollinger Bands)    
      We continue our journey of discovering the fundamental indicators of technical analysis with Bollinger Bands. Essential tool to keep track of market  volatility  , this last one is set by two bands (a high one and a low one) with a middle line between them. When the upper and lower lines move away from the middle line, market volatility can be expected to increase (expansion phase). Otherwise, the convergence of the two bands indicates a contraction phase. When the Bollinger Bands follow the median path, it is possible that the market is going through a period of stability and relative consolidation. Finally, should the BBs contract too much - almost to the point of breaking the central line - the market of your interest could be dominated by an unpredictable reversal that changes the cryptocurrency's value in almost "explosive" ways.

      We would also like to draw your attention to the fact that the price of a currency even has the opportunity to cross the two outer bands (both high and low). Should they be breached, the crypto market would experience a period of extreme contraction or expansion.     
      It follows that Bollinger Bands are an important tool in assessing altcoin  short positions  - although they should not be construed as a determining factor in your TA.  Our advice?  Postpone opening your market position during periods when excessive market volatility risks invalidating your crypto purchase in record time.     
            
      05 – Moving Average Convergence/Divergence (MACD)    
      The MACD - an acronym for Moving Average Convergence-Divergence Indicator - is TA's newest ally for gaining an optimal understanding of market dynamics. It was theorized by Gerald Appel in the 1970s and owes its success to the ability to determine with great accuracy the  dynamics of a market and possible trends in value.  As if that were not enough, the entry and exit points so valued by speculators are also highlighted.     
      Let's go in order.    
      Understanding the MACD is inseparable from the above moving averages. And indeed, the moving average convergence divergence indicator is generated by the difference between two EMAs (Exponential Moving Averages) to form a line relative to the previous ones. The latter bears the name of the signal line. The distribution of the EMA always oscillates around a median value (the zero line) and converges in a histogram - as shown in  Figure 8  .     
            
         
      Figure 8 - The tripartition of the MACD. Source: Mt5traders    
            
      In detail:     
     
       The  MACD  #1 provides information about up and down trends in a market and is calculated from the difference between two EMAs.     
       The  Signal Line  (line #2) is an EMA derived from MACD #1, the combined analysis of which can help traders identify potential points for opening and closing profitable investment positions. In most cases, when the MACD and the signal line intersect, a trend reversal occurs in the market. This scenario is even more true when the crossing occurs at the edge of the chart - ie away from the zero line in the middle.     
       Finally, the histogram (line #3) represents the divergence and convergence moves that exist between the MACD and the signal line. In other words, line number 3 is measured by the difference between the other two lines. For the obligation of the record, this last one is presented in the form of bar chart to facilitate reading and analysis. Also, please note that the individual "candles" that make up the histogram have nothing to do with the trading volume of the exchange platforms. However, you can use the histogram to your advantage in the following ways: When the range approaches the zero line, your altcoin is in a period of low volatility: the price is in a downtrend and is very likely to enter a bearish or bullish trend "explode". This behavior is called  histogram squeeze  .     
     
      Finally, don't forget that the time frame considered by the MACD is 100% customizable: it is not uncommon to create analysis periods of 12 or 26 days, as well as daily analysis, extremely useful for investors taking short positions. For these reasons, the MACD is also known as a  trend-following indicator  .     
      Now we can almost imagine what is going through your mind:  how to read the lines of the moving average divergence convergence indicator?  As the name suggests, it is easy to see that the measurable value of the MACD is related to the divergence or convergence of the two lines (number 1 and number 2).  In the first case they diverge from each other, in the second case they converge.  Again, the most effective trading strategy is one that focuses on the divergence and convergence of the MACD and the signal line to predict potential stock market moves.     
      Conclusion  : When the MACD crosses the signal line with an uptrend (up), traders can open profitable long positions. On the other hand, when lines 1 and 2 cross in a downtrend (down), it is better to consider selling cryptocurrencies and closing the position.

      Chapter 7  Reading order books (and how to use them to minimize risks)      
            
      Dear Reader  , In this chapter, we'd like to take a quick step back to discuss how an exchange platform works. Of course, you can also find out more on YouTube, Reddit or Facebook, but we believe that reading an order book is inseparable from the technical analysis that you have become familiar with on the previous pages.Therefore, in addition to explaining how you can trade, we will also focus on the importance of the bid-ask spread and slippage of a  short  or  long  position. The goal? Deepen the dynamics of supply and demand so you can further reduce the risks associated with your crypto experience.     
      Firstly, what is an order book? The  order book  is the electronic listing of accumulation and distribution movements associated with a crypto asset according to its value. In other words: The speculative interests of investors (almost) always manifest themselves dynamically and, above all, in real time in a 100% digital order book. The number of orders is called the  market depth  , while the average time between the meeting of the ask and the bid (supply and demand) indicates the  liquidity of the market  . Please note that each  pair  of cryptocurrencies and/or fiat currencies has its own financial microcosm for reference. Therefore, the information we have summarized on the following pages should be applied at the end of a successful fundamental and technical analysis - after finding the altcoin of your interest.     
      The  transparency  of an order book is a sine qua non for the security of your crypto trading. Almost all exchange platforms currently online have order books for traditional bonds, assets, fiat currencies, stocks, bitcoin and altcoin. In addition, the above information is visible to anyone accessing the investment platform to buy, sell or follow market fluctuations. And indeed, the daily update of the order books allows to study the market sentiment and the financial behavior of other active users.     
      The dynamics of an  order book  are reflected in 4 macro areas:     
     
       Buy orders  contain information about potential buyers, including the amount one wishes to amass the cryptocurrency for (and more).     
       Sell  orders  submit information about the sellers and the price at which the altcoin is to be distributed.     
       Volume  refers to the amount of cryptocurrency that active members are willing to sell and/or buy on the exchange.     
       Finally, at the top of the order book, the  maximum and minimum bid prices  are displayed.     
     
            
           
           
           
           
           
      Figure 16 - Example of an order book on Binance. Source: Binance    
           
      Dear reader  , when you buy or sell cryptocurrencies on an exchange platform, you trade in accordance with the demand (  ask  ) and the supply (  bid  ) of a reference market. So ,  price  plays an important role in determining wins and losses. However, don't make the mistake of underestimating a long, very long list of secondary factors that contribute to the nature of the market. We refer to liquidity, depth, volume and the types of orders that are placed in real-time. Depending on the more or less advantageous combination of the above elements, you will receive buy and sell prices with a profit or loss.     
      Also, since the trading experience is based on  constant negotiations between sellers and potential buyers  , you need to be aware of the difference between the bid and ask price. This value is known as the  bid-ask spread  and plays an important role in determining your financial behavior.     
      The  Binance Academy definition [9]  reads as follows:     
      “An asset's bid-ask spread is the difference  between the highest bid price and the lowest ask price in an order book. [...] If you want to make an instant purchase at the market price, you must accept a seller's lowest ask price. If you choose to make an instant sale, you will receive the highest bid price of any buyer.

Otherwise, you run the risk of waiting hours - or even days - for your cryptocurrency to be sold. We use this term to denote an intermediary who buys and sells a large quantity of crypto assets in order  to increase the liquidity of a market  and  to maintain the exchange between supply and demand  . Well, the market makers act on their own: it's not uncommon for them to be able to make sizeable profits on the bid-ask spread of a crypto  pair  simply by selling the highs and buying the lows multiple times in a row. Even with small  bid  - ask  spreads, the concerned investors can realize a capital gain because they are able to move large amounts of currency.

      Positive and negative slippage: how to fight back?      
      slippage  . This phenomenon is particularly common in markets with low liquidity or very high volatility. For this reason, it is the undisputed protagonist of small-cap altcoin exchanges. What is this made of? Cryptocurrencies are bought and sold  at a price other than the price originally set .  Imagine you want to buy an alternative coin for $200 and know that the current price is $200/$201. They then offer to pay for the asset at a price of $201. However, in the few seconds that you place an order and wait for your  bid  to reach the  Exchange platform servers  , the price of the currency changes or your internet connection slows down so much that you open a position with a slight delay.     
      Don't be surprised:  we've already told you how closely an order book tracks value fluctuations in real time, right? As a result, you are forced to buy the lot you are interested in at a price of $201.7. The $0.7 difference is called  slippage  while its numerical value is calculated in  points  or  ticks  .    
      Furthermore, the successful processing of an order by the exchange platform requires that there are two parties willing to carry out the transaction at the same price:  the buyer and the bidder . In other words, if we want to buy 10 lots of an altcoin at $50 each, we need to make sure there is enough market availability to meet our demand. It is possible that our demand is only partially covered. In this case we buy the available lots at the agreed price (e.g. 8 out of 10) and the rest at a higher price accompanied by some  points/ticks (2 out of 10 sold at an example value of 50.3 USD).     
      If we only look at it from this point of view, we fall into the trap of believing that slippage  is only  negative. However, if we work on broker platforms that offer both positive and negative (symmetric) slippages, we can use this situation to our advantage to further profit from the altcoins in our wallet. But how? Let's imagine we place an order with a market price of 50.164, but find that the price of the altcoin drops to 50.110 right at the moment when we want to buy the cryptocurrency. In such a scenario, we have an opportunity to collect a lot of currencies at a better price than originally set and collect a positive slippage of 0.064 ticks. The example above is intentionally "exaggerated" numerically to help you understand how the slippage mechanism works. However, we can assure you that even just 2 tick points is more than enough to make you happy with your trading action. While not very common,  slippage profits occur  almost exclusively in small-cap altcoin markets that are characterized by high volatility.     
      On the other hand, if you fear that you might become a victim of negative slippage, we recommend you to take an investment position on exchange platforms with a  Tolerance  function [10]  to open. The purpose of the tolerance is to slow down or speed up the processing of your request. In other words, if you set the slippage tolerance low, you enter the market more slowly - with the risk that you only make partial purchases. On the other hand, if you set the slippage tolerance too high, you might encounter other traders (or bots) interested in selling the position  in front running.  This term refers to the practice - primarily used in the Ethereum ecosystem - of identifying potentially profitable stocks opened by other users and spending a larger amount of gas to anticipate them and thus make a profit. The front runner then places a new order at a higher price. This strategy, considered illegal in the traditional stock market, comes with higher costs for the crypto investor as they are forced to pay for their asset of interest the higher price they are willing to spend  (corresponding to the slippage mentioned above -Tolerance).    
      Therefore, to minimize the risk of negative slippage, you can:     
     
       Splitting a large order into smaller mini-orders to complete the purchase as a whole.     
       Use so -called  limit orders  . Limit orders are a category of standalone orders that are sold and/or bought  at a fixed price .  All you have to do is set the minimum and maximum value at which you want to buy or sell your cryptocurrencies and put yourself on hold. Your order will be placed in the exchange's order book and will only be executed if the market conditions match the conditions you previously set. The advantage? You don't need to update your exchange platform in real-time as the  limit order  feature is available 24/7. Sure, you need to say goodbye to the immediacy of executing a position instantly, but at least you can rest assured that you won't suffer any nasty negative slippages on your crypto wallet. It's worth the wait.  There's no harm in trying!    
       Finally, add the monthly number of negative slippages to the equity you want to invest in altcoin. Since slippage is almost inevitable, at Mindful Finance we suggest that you calculate weekly and/or monthly values and organize them in charts or simple ledgers to predict the average slippage price that you "lose" during the implementation of your trading project " have to.     
     
      One can never be too careful, and in time you will understand how to avoid the easily predictable phenomena of slippage and front running.

In general, a precise definition of “  risk  ” refers to fluctuations in the value of your crypto assets. An example? Small-cap currency A is a much riskier investment than large-cap currency B. Additionally, cryptocurrency A's liquidity is bound to be lower than cryptocurrency B's, further increasing the overall unpredictability of your purchases. Furthermore, the notion of risk can take on another negative connotation, this time related to the amount of money you're looking to amass. So imagine you have a wallet with 1 BTC. If you decide to invest 0.1% of your capital in altcoin, you expose yourself to a 10% risk. In other words, you are putting 1/10th of your finances in an asset that is inherently volatile and unpredictable. Our  risk management  approach takes into account the two factors mentioned above. However, we would like to point out that the risk of investing in low-caps or high-caps can be significantly reduced by keeping an eye on the total value of a wallet  to avoid risky moves  .     
      Now, before we delve into complex risk management issues, we want to reiterate a concept that has been mentioned several times in previous chapters: invest  only  as much money in cryptocurrencies as you are willing to lose. Wrong investments cause emotional trauma, which is better to stay away from: it makes you lose confidence in your own decision-making abilities [12]  and encourage totally irrational and harmful economic behavior. Throughout our crypto trading careers, we all started by investing a few hundred dollars. The latter is a perfectly legitimate "financial framework" because it allows the advice contained in the previous chapters to be applied objectively, sanely and serenely. Serenity is the sine qua non for anyone who decides to follow the path of crypto economy.     
      We're very fortunate today: we don't have to be New York traders to participate in today's big financial changes. Cryptocurrencies are democratic: they allow you to get acquainted with the investment experience without panicking and without "gambling away" your salary. All too often, new investors dive into the world of cryptoassets by reading guide after guide in hopes that they will fill in the gaps that their lack of experience provides. In reality, the movement of 100-200 EUR in altcoin is the only really successful way. Only then will you figure out what strategies work for you and how to deal with moments of high market volatility without becoming a victim of FOMO.     
      We ask you: are you really interested in the world of cryptocurrency? If the answer is yes, be bold, open your first wallet and start buying an altcoin that piques your curiosity and meets the analytical requirements of the Fundamental and Technical Analysis. On the other hand, if you don't believe in the crypto economy and want to try your hand at buying a penthouse villa in downtown Milan without lifting a finger, put your financial ambitions aside and invest your money wisely. As in so many areas of professional and emotional life, only those who believe in the project Satoshi Nakamoto started over a decade ago will thrive in the world of Bitcoin and altcoin.     
      The most important thing is to sleep well. Always.    
      There is not much to add to the first definition of risk. We have already explained that low-cap currencies are characterized by their inherent volatility, which is variable and unpredictable. The latter type of altcoin - the one we love most at Mindful Finance - puts you at significantly higher risk. However, we want you to think about an aspect that many advanced traders continue to underestimate. Pay close attention to what we're about to tell you, because it will make all the difference: small-cap cryptocurrencies, while lacking in liquidity and security,  are most profitable during bear markets  .     
      In a sudden bear market scenario, the mid- and high-cap altcoin's seeming stability is unraveling in record time. Behind the illusory solidity of a wallet in BTC, ETH and so on lies a significant investment fog. In fact, the biggest devaluations are seen among the major crypto financial companies. The low-cap and micro-cap stocks that interest you, on the other hand, remain particularly stable during the most turbulent market phases. This happens mainly for two reasons: A) The low liquidity of small cap altcoin  forces  traders to keep their positions unchanged. In short, it is much easier to disrupt the balance of the Bitcoin market than in a narrower cryptoeconomic scenario - i.e. where the buying and selling phases are slower and more tedious. B) In the vast majority of cases, investors buy small shares of small-cap altcoins. Indeed, it would not make sense to differentiate one's wallet by buying more than 50% micro-cap or low-cap. The result? Seized by fears of a bear market, most investors will seek to sell or buy Bitcoin and the predominant altcoins in their wallets in hopes of "saving the day." This setting, in turn, guarantees lower volatility of the low caps in bear market phases.     
      As for the practical side of things, at Mindful Finance we prefer altcoin trading with a  firm risk management . By this we mean an investment scenario where each cryptocurrency occupies a certain percentage in our wallet. Micro caps have a  fixed  risk between 0-1%, low caps between 1 and 3% and mid caps between 3 and 5%. This means that each currency - depending on its classification - will have a maximum share of 0 to 5% in our altcoin wallet. Not one percentage point more. In addition, the term “  fixed ”  implies that the cryptocurrencies we hold are held in a  long position  , with  no stop loss in place  .     
      Craziness? Under no circumstance.

The entry price looks promising, market sentiment is good and the market has very good liquidity. We decide to open a position on a smaller exchange platform to minimize negative slippage. Should the buy of low-cap A  be delayed  , we will continue to calculate the buy percentage (3%) relative to the value of BTC held at the time of the first  position  . We will hold our altcoin A until its market cap starts to increase or its value goes into free fall - that is, when we realize we can make or lose money. In the (unfortunate) event that the low-cap price hits 0, we know we've lost 3% of our money without having to use complicated stop-loss systems.     
      The reason for this?     
      The stop-loss proves pretty useless in extreme volatility of low and mid-cap altcoins.

      The 2% rule for determining the risk of action      
      Dear reader  , at Mindful Finance we are very meticulous and don't want to leave anything to chance. Although the above strategy is the one we think is the best in terms of altcoin risk management, we would like to open a short informative parenthesis about the  2% rule  .  Have you ever heard of this?  Used primarily in the traditional financial world, it states that a trader should not bet more than 2% of their total holdings on a brand new (and therefore potentially losing) asset.     
      Now we can almost imagine what's going through your mind: «  Isn't that the exact opposite of what we explained in the previous paragraph?  ». Apparently yes, but with some very needed clarifications. Unlike tangible and intangible assets that can be bought on the traditional market, bitcoin and altcoin follow faster and more unpredictable market cycles. The high volatility of investments makes it impossible to determine the success (or failure) of any single action. This does not mean that you should only move 1% of your available capital, but that - in conditions of pathological uncertainty - you would do well to place a stop loss at 1-2% of your investment.     
      This is true for mid and large cap cryptocurrencies, especially if you are a beginner and want to make your wallet as heterogeneous as possible.     
      Please note that a stop loss is an  invalid point , a numeric value where we say our idea was wrong and we need to take action to avoid burning more money. In short, while there is no one-size-fits-all approach to traders' stop-loss and risk management strategies, we recommend adopting the two approaches above:     
     
       Invest up to 1% of the capital with micro-caps without stop-loss     
       For low caps 1-3% without stop loss     
       For mid-caps from 3/5% with no stop loss     
       For high caps, different percentages of your wallet with stop loss at 1-2% (depending on your trading strategies).

      Cognitive Distortions and Bias - When the mind misleads us      
      Dear reader  , take a deep breath. In the previous section we put a lot of meat on the fire with the intention of giving you all our (mental) trading secrets. This raises the question of the extent to which human  judgment  plays a crucial role in determining financial behavior. Or perhaps we should speak of  predestination .  Yes, because the seat of our logical-rational intelligence is anything but infallible. The brain works with complex identification processes.     
      Imagine you are walking down the street on a beautiful spring Sunday. Suddenly a crowd starts running in the opposite direction. The strangers have a frightened expression on their faces and give the impression that they are running away from  something  or  someone .  What happens? The primitive part of your mind - " Old Brain  " or " Primitive Brain  " in English - is activated on the spot and sends out an alarm signal. And so, without you realizing it, all your  “rational  ”  mental activities recede  into the background. You no longer bother with your email from work or the family dinner, but instead run for your life from an enemy you don't know but who is probably nearby. In other words, the mechanism of the primitive brain is capable of absorbing your full attention and, in response to an external stimulus (a crowd of people running towards you, a scary spider in the garden, a car moving at full speed, etc. ) to trigger a life-saving process.     
      When the Old Brain comes into play, there is no longer any logical-rational motivation: we follow our survival instincts  without inhibitions  and without  hesitation  .     
      Why did we tell you this short story?    
      Very easily.     
      The primitive brain plays a very important role in market psychology. There are in fact several automatic and 100% unconscious  shortcuts in thinking  that lead us down the wrong financial path. These are  cognitive  biases, the same ones that marketers, advertisers, and businesses use to attract desire and sell a product and/or service: their own. Such brain paradigms lead people down the path of irrationality, regardless of their level of experience or risk tolerance. The moment the Primitive Brain appears in an altcoin's life cycle, technical analysis is obsolete.     
      This explains why, at Mindful Finance, we have devoted a particularly extensive section of our manual to the cyclical nature of a crypto market.     
      Some examples of cognitive bias are in detail:     
     
       The endowment  effect is shorthand for a mindset that aims to overvalue what you own. Every altcoin trader is driven to overstate a cryptocurrency's success simply by the fact that they've accumulated it.  The same goes for NFTs (Non-Fungible Tokens) and stablecoins!    
       Fear of  loss  is one of the most insidious enemies of crypto beginners. It's a curious mental tendency: people fear loss more than they enjoy gain, whether the gain is greater or less than the loss. This cognitive bias has a strong impact on the emotional perception of the market, especially at times when traders are panicking and trying to close their positions underwater.     
       Confirmation Bias  is a great contemporary classic. It can be observed in all forms of communication (especially social networks) and alludes to the general tendency to give more credence to information that  seems to confirm one's thesis.  In other words, an investor in a bull market places more faith in positive news and ignores signals that indicate a trend reversal.  The risk is going into the bear market unprepared.    
     
      In summary, the vast majority of speculators no doubt believe in the importance of market psychology - but often lack a rigorous enough analysis scheme to navigate the jungle of human emotions. At Mindful Finance, we've tried to fill this gap -  a gap that particularly affects the Italian crypto panorama  - because we firmly believe in the power of the human brain. A prime example of this was the bursting of the  dot-com bubble  in the 1990s; a setback that had devastating effects in America and Europe and brought many progressive speculators to their knees. This is not meant to demoralize you, but rather to alert you to a truth with a capital W: it is very difficult for traders to differentiate between real market opportunities and opportunities for enrichment created by the cognitive biases mentioned above. Our way is to remind you to rely on the cyclical phases shown in  Figure 18  and to question all of your feelings with the right critical mind.     
      Sure, you will take the risk of buying and selling altcoin more slowly, but you will learn the hard way that patience is every successful speculator's trump card.     
      We warned you.

However, the procedure is quite simple and only requires some basic knowledge of how an exchange platform and order book works.  So what to do?  First and foremost, we recommend you get a few excellent allies: the  Blockchain Explorer rich list  to anticipate the financial behavior of the most active addresses,  support and resistance  levels to predict potential trend reversals, and  market psychology  to understand when to close your position at a profit.     
      Again, your strategy is a matter of timing!    
      Personally, we'd advise against waiting too long in hopes of hitting the peak of euphoria. The reason? While you can get the most out of your investment, you also run the risk of getting rid of the coins too slowly - ie in an inevitable bear market. Remember that trader attention and interest are very volatile - just like the markets. There's no point in aiming for the jackpot if you can't control the pace of buying and selling. Gain experience and become aware of your skills, learn to appreciate every little improvement, and remember that the ultimate goal of the new trader is not to win easily, it is not to lose so easily.

      Let's go step by step and try to understand what are the macro categories of altcoin and token available in the current market.     
      Platform tokens  are hybrid  alternative  coins generated by specific multifunctional blockchains. Such is the case with Ethereum, where Ether, the platform's native token, has slowly become the first altcoin by market cap. The philosophy behind these platforms is successful for at least two reasons: first, because users have the ability to create scripts (such as smart contracts) to facilitate the exchange of values in a decentralized way, and second, because the platform Tokens enjoy high visibility on crypto exchanges, attracting the attention of a highly diverse audience. Besides the already mentioned Ethereum, we recommend you to take a look at Solana (SOL) and Polkadot (DOT). The former was launched in March 2020 with the intention of introducing a new validation system [13]  while the latter dates back to 2017 and was developed by Ethereum co-founders Gavin Wood, Peter Czaban and Robert Habermeier. We conclude our compilation of platform tokens with BNB (Binance Chain), which - after a successful crowdfunding on Vitalik Buterin's platform - launched its own blockchain network in September 2020.     
      Meme  coins are the category of altcoin that we at Mindful Finance are wise to avoid.  Do you have to do that too? Under no circumstance!  Our reservations hinge on high market volatility and uninviting payout earnings. For the record, meme coins are the result of digital trends that are very elusive financially. The best example is Dogecoin (DOGE), whose mining and rewarding process we analyzed in previous chapters.Founded in December 2013 with the intention of parodying “stable” cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin, Dogecoin has since achieved a large market cap and even garnered the approval of Elon Musk himself. But let's be honest: investing in DOGE and similar currencies does not mean supporting crypto projects with a futuristic and revolutionary character, but rather participating in viral phenomena that - as so often - involve the risk that one has a thorn in his hand. So there is nothing to stop you from investing in an altcoin meme that follows the above advice. However, be careful and trade with low stop losses (1%).     
      DeFi  (Decentralized Finance)  tokens  have a working mechanism that is diametrically opposed to simply hoarding altcoin in your crypto wallet. The reason? The currencies in question are used for  staking, governance  and  profit sharing  . If these terms don't sound familiar to you, consider the following: The decentralization of a blockchain network applies to a variety of professional, institutional, and corporate domains. In the area of government (  governance  ), blockchain technology preserves the integrity of data and the transparency of communication exchanges at national and international levels. It follows that the Decentralized Finance branch of research wants to reorganize the management of banking institutions, hierarchical infrastructures and all other service providers (whether public or private) through the native working mechanisms of the blockchain. To achieve this, the intelligent automatisms anchored in the Smart Contracts are used extensively.     
      Now what does it mean to invest in DeFi tokens?    
      The idea behind Decentralized Finance is to create intermediary-free scenarios in which individual users can influence project decisions and activities through the use of tokens. Stripped of the aura of passivity of ordinary altcoin, the tokens become “entry tickets” to actually intervene in the affairs of the de-fi initiative one is a part of.  Here is an example.  Although the subject is complex and ever-changing - in  short, to exhaust the subject we would have to write a book specifically about blockchain networks in the De-Fi space!  - we recommend you to look into UNI, the Uniswap token, and CAKE, the PancakeSwap token. Both proposals grant voting rights to individual users in proportion to the number of UNI or CAKE they own. The projects are therefore part of  decentralized democratic governance  - an area where users can accept or reject the network's proposals by expressing their opinions using tokens.     
      We close with  Game-Fi tokens  , currencies with a gaming character that are gaining a foothold in the blockchain technology environment at a time of profound viral changes. Gambling platforms based on blockchain technology are not a new invention, but were born in the "distant" 2013. Their operation is inseparable from the possession of Non-Fungible Tokens (NFT), which offer investors the opportunity to buy characters, equip them with armour, upgrades and bonus abilities, and bet on the outcome of battles/games. Again, Game-Fi tokens fall outside the scope of the trading strategies mentioned above. One thing is for sure, the NFT market is constantly on the rise, and there is momentum that bodes well for both creators and buyers. We don't want to provide you with information that's easy to find on the web, but if you're interested in the universe of 100% decentralized art or gaming, you can log on to  OpenSea  , today's largest crypto marketplace, to take a look to check out the current offers.
